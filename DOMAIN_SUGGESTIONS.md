## Setup Process (After Purchase)

### 1. Buy Domain (~5 minutes)
- Go to Namecheap/Cloudflare/Google Domains
- Search for your chosen domain
- Purchase (usually $12-40/year)
- Enable privacy protection (free on most)

### 2. Authenticate with SendGrid (~15 minutes)
1. Go to: https://app.sendgrid.com/settings/sender_auth
2. Click "Authenticate Your Domain"
3. Enter your domain (e.g., `safealert.io`)
4. Copy the DNS records SendGrid provides
5. Add them to your domain registrar's DNS settings
6. Wait 24-48 hours for DNS propagation
7. Verify in SendGrid

### 3. Update Render Environment Variables
```env
FROM_EMAIL=notifications@safealert.io
APP_DOMAIN=https://safealert.io
```

### 4. Point Domain to Render (Optional)
If you want `safealert.io` to show your app:
1. In Render dashboard, go to your service
2. Click "Settings" → "Custom Domain"
3. Add your domain
4. Update DNS records at your registrar
5. Enable HTTPS (automatic with Render)
